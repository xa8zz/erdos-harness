### Independent professional critique (single credentialed source over 1.5+ years)

**[Will Barker (Cambridge / Czech Academy)]** arXiv:2311.11790 (Nov 2023, just one month after Partanen-Tulkki v1):

> *"We examine the weak-field, zero-coupling limit of Yang–Mills gravity as recently formulated by Partanen and Tulkki, viewed as a free quantum field theory. In this approximation the theory has a ghostly teleparallel vacuum. We suggest that bimetric, vacuum expectation value, or finite-coupling extensions should be investigated."*

This is the **only credentialed third-party arxiv critique** found over 1.5+ years of public record. Barker is a serious quantum-gravity researcher (Cambridge PhD, multiple papers on Poincaré gauge theory, metric-affine gravity, ghost spectra in modified gravity).

Barker's broader 2024–2025 work develops the same machinery (spectral analysis of gauge gravity theories, ghost / strong-coupling diagnostics) on related but distinct theories — Poincaré gauge theory (arXiv:2406.12826), metric-affine gravity (arXiv:2505.23894), parity-violating gauge gravity (arXiv:2506.02111), R² gravity Hamiltonian (arXiv:2510.08201) — but does **not** appear to have published a follow-up rebuttal specifically targeting Partanen-Tulkki since the Nov 2023 paper.

## How the program's results compare

### Where R02–R04 go beyond the public record

1. **Specific one-loop paper-internal subtraction defect (R02-pro-2 + R03-pro1).** The chain — κ p⁴/ε_UV 1PI pole + constant-Z_H bare counterterm + nonpolynomial δZ_H^(1)(p²) momentum-dependent subtraction = either new higher-derivative counterterm enlarging the action or nonlocal subtraction outside BPHZ — with paper-internal equation citations (157, 161, 162, 187–189, 196, 203–205) is **not present in the public record**. Casadio-Kuntz-Paci is at a higher level of generality (teleparallel matter loops, not specifically Partanen-Tulkki's δZ_H prescription). Barker's critique is at a different level (spectrum of the propagator, not the counterterm-basis-vs-divergence-structure mismatch). The program's R02-pro-2 + R03-pro1 result appears to be a fresh observation.

2. **Structural impossibility statement (R04).** No public source articulates the impossibility result "no local-BPHZ-renormalizable descendant can keep exact U(1)^4 + TEGR closure + finite couplings + all three stated goals simultaneously." The community discourse acknowledges "1-loop not proven all-orders" and "needs further work," but does not frame this as a structural impossibility.

3. **Two-descendant trade-off map (R04).** No public source explicitly maps the (i)-vs-(ii) trade-off between EFT-style completion and quadratic-gravity-with-fakeons. The community has the pieces — Donoghue/Burgess EFT, Salvio quadratic gravity, Anselmi-Piva fakeons, Casadio-Kuntz-Paci teleparallel matter loops — but the program is, as far as the canvass shows, the first to weld them into a single structural verdict applied to unified gravity.

### Where the public record is ahead

1. **Barker's ghost finding (arXiv:2311.11790).** The program's R02–R04 did not analyze the spin spectrum of the propagator at the weak-field zero-coupling limit. Barker did, and found a ghost mode. **This is compatible with R03-pro2** ("the action is dynamical and produces a propagator") but adds that the propagator's pole structure contains a negative-norm mode. It also dovetails with R04's quadratic-gravity descendant (which has a Stelle ghost unless fakeon-quantized) — but Barker's finding suggests the ghost may be present already in the BASE unified-gravity construction, not only in a Stelle-style enlargement.

2. **Authors' own follow-up trajectory.** arXiv:2507.07790 (graviton-graviton extension, tree level) explicitly enlarges the bare action while preserving 4×U(1) gauge invariance. This is consistent with R04's prediction that the bare action would need enlargement — specifically, it moves slightly toward the EFT descendant (adding non-linear gravity-gravity coupling) without addressing the loop-level critique. arXiv:2505.14446 (light deflection, tree-level) claims 23–27% deviation from GR at second-PN-order — if accurate, that's a strong testable prediction; if not, that's an internal inconsistency. Both papers stay at tree level.

3. **Second corrigendum (Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 079501, 2025).** Surfaced in this canvass but not yet fetched; could affect specific equation references used by R02–R04.
