## Background

The paper "Gravity generated by four one-dimensional unitary gauge symmetries and the Standard Model" by Mikko Partanen and Jukka Tulkki (Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 057802, 2025; arXiv:2310.01460) presents a U(1)^4 gauge theory of gravity ("unified gravity") and claims it is one-loop renormalizable in the standard Bogoliubov-Parasiuk-Hepp-Zimmermann sense, in the same manner as Yang-Mills theory with compact gauge group on a Minkowski background. The task below evaluates a specific reading of the paper's one-loop renormalization procedure.

## Claim to verify or refute

Consider the following structural reading of the paper's one-loop renormalization of the gravitational sector. The reading consists of three factual claims about the paper's actual equations, plus an interpretive claim about their implications.

**Factual claims about the paper:**

1. The paper's one-loop graviton self-energy 1PI two-point function (denoted Ξ_{1L} or its equivalent in the paper's notation) contains an ultraviolet-divergent term whose overall momentum scaling, after extraction of the transverse projector structure, is κ p^4 / ε_UV (where ε_UV is the dimensional-regularization parameter and κ = 8π G is the Einstein gravitational constant). It is **not** the p^2 scaling that would correspond to a standard Yang-Mills-style wave-function renormalization.

2. The paper's bare-action counterterm structure for absorbing this 1PI two-point divergence is restricted to (Z_H − 1) p^2 P̂, where Z_H is a renormalization factor for the gravitational gauge field H and P̂ is the transverse projector. The factor Z_H is intended in the bare Lagrangian as a constant — independent of external momentum p^2.

3. The paper resolves the apparent incompatibility between a κ p^4 / ε_UV divergence and a constant-Z_H counterterm by introducing a renormalization factor δZ_H^(1) that is itself momentum-dependent: explicitly a function of p^2, the electron mass squared m_e^2, the ratios m_e^2 / p^2 and m_e^4 / p^4, and logarithms of these quantities. The momentum dependence is required to satisfy the subtraction conditions Ξ_{1L}|_{p^2 = 0} = 0 and (Ξ_{1L} / p^4)|_{p^2 → ∞} = 0.

**Interpretive claim about the BPHZ status of (3):**

4. The pole part of a momentum-dependent renormalization factor δZ_H^(1)(p^2) corresponds, in the renormalized action, to a new four-derivative gauge-invariant counterterm of the schematic form H'_{a μ ν} □ H'^{a μ ν} (mass dimension 6 under canonical dimensions [H] = 1, [∂] = 1) which is **not** present in the bare Lagrangian under the original parameter set. Equivalently, the nonpolynomial momentum dependence of δZ_H^(1)(p^2), taken globally rather than just its pole part, corresponds to a nonlocal subtraction outside the BPHZ class. Either interpretation invalidates the Yang-Mills-style renormalizability claim.
