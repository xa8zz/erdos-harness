## What is established

A candidate quantum theory of gravity ("unified gravity") was proposed by Partanen and Tulkki in arXiv:2310.01460 (Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 057802, 2025; two published corrigenda at Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 069501, 2025 — factor-of-2 fix in projector tensor prefactor across five equations including eq. 162; and 88, 079501, 2025 — eq. 122 factor 1/2 → 1, no downstream impact). The construction is a U(1)^4 abelian gauge theory with eight-spinor matter coupling and a teleparallel-superpotential kinetic term L_{g,kin} = (1/4κ) H_{ρμν} S^{ρμν} √(-g), claimed to be one-loop renormalizable in the standard Bogoliubov-Parasiuk-Hepp-Zimmermann sense.

Multiple converging lines of analysis establish that the headline renormalizability claim does not hold within the strict original parameter set. The specific obstructions are:

**Obstruction A — matter-loop higher-derivative and four-fermion counterterms.** Spinor matter loops generate a Weyl-squared / four-derivative (∂F)^2 counterterm of mass dimension 6 in the gauge variables, with Duff coefficient b = 1/20 per Dirac fermion (Duff, *Twenty years of the Weyl anomaly*, Class. Quantum Grav. 11, 1387, 1994). The Einstein-Dirac one-loop sector additionally generates an irreducible dimension-12 four-fermion counterterm proportional to (ψ̄ γ^a γ_5 ψ)^4 (Deser & van Nieuwenhuizen, *Nonrenormalizability of the quantized Dirac-Einstein system*, Phys. Rev. D 10, 411, 1974). Both are BRST-closed in ghost-number-0 cohomology (Barnich-Brandt-Henneaux, *Local BRST cohomology in gauge theories*, Phys. Rep. 338, 439-569, 2000; arXiv:hep-th/0002245) and absent from the bare Lagrangian.

**Obstruction B — paper-internal counterterm-basis mismatch.** The one-loop graviton self-energy 1PI two-point tensor (eqs. 187-188 of arXiv:2310.01460v11) has overall scaling κ p^4 / ε_UV with the electron loop contributing (i κ c p^4 / (480 π² ℏ)) × [1/ε_UV + log(4π μ² e^{-γ} / m_e^2 c^2)] P̂_B and the photon loop contributing (i κ c p^4 / (240 π² ℏ)) × [...] P̂_{3,2,1}, with divergent decomposition into tensor projectors P̂_{3,2,1} − 10 m_e^2 c^2 p^{-2} P̂_{1,2,1} − 30 m_e^4 c^4 p^{-4} P_{1,0,1}. The bare-action counterterm structure (eqs. 157, 161, 162, 189) is restricted to i (Z_H − 1) p^2 P̂_{1,2,1} with constant Z_H. The paper's resolution introduces a momentum-dependent δZ_H^{(1)}(p^2) (eqs. 203-205) satisfying Ξ_{1L}|_{p^2 = 0} = 0 and (Ξ_{1L} / p^4)|_{p^2 → ∞} = 0, with explicit nonpolynomial dependence on p^2, m_e^2 / p^2, m_e^4 / p^4 and logarithms. The structural reading is that this corresponds either to enlargement of the local counterterm basis with new higher-derivative gauge-invariant operators (e.g. H'_{aμν} □ H'^{aμν}, mass dimension 6) beyond the original parameter set — exiting power-counting renormalizability — or to nonlocal propagator-level subtraction outside the BPHZ class.

**Obstruction C — teleparallel matter-loop divergences outside the TEGR one-parameter family.** Per Casadio, Kuntz, Paci (*Quantum fields in teleparallel gravity: renormalization at one-loop*, Eur. Phys. J. C 82, 262, 2022; arXiv:2110.04325), quantum matter loops on a teleparallel background generate one-loop divergences with structure depending on coupling prescription: under full equivalence-principle coupling, divergences are quartic in torsion; under gauge-only translation coupling, divergences are quadratic in torsion in the Yang-Mills contraction T^{ρμν} T_{ρμν} alone, not in the TEGR linear combination (1/4) T^{ρμν} T_{ρμν} + (1/2) T^{ρμν} T_{νμρ} − T^{ρ}_{μρ} T^{νμ}_{ν}. Since the bare TEGR sector has only one gravitational coupling (Newton's constant), absorption requires enlargement to a three-parameter "New GR" teleparallel action with independent coefficients for the three torsion-quadratic invariants.

BRST does not save the claim: the offending counterterms are local, Lorentz-invariant, gauge-invariant, and BRST-closed in ghost-number-0 cohomology; the algebraic-renormalization framework allows them. The construction's "dimensionless gauge coupling" is recovered only after a √κ field rescaling that hides the dimensionful coupling in the interaction vertices, giving the effective expansion parameter α_g = G p^2 / (ℏ c^3) — energy-scale-dependent, characteristic of effective field theory rather than fixed BPHZ renormalizability.

Within the strict original family — local algebraic-renormalization framework with U(1)^4 abelian gauge structure, exact TEGR closure under renormalization, and a finite bare coupling set — no local-BPHZ-renormalizable descendant can absorb A, B, C while preserving all three stated goals of the program: (i) perturbative renormalizability on the same footing as the Standard Model interactions, (ii) classical reduction to general relativity via the TEGR geometric equivalence, (iii) compatibility with the equivalence principle through equality of renormalized inertial and gravitational masses. Two coherent descendants exist within known physics: a teleparallel/tetrad effective field theory with full local higher-derivative + torsion-quadratic + torsion-quartic + four-fermion operator basis (preserves classical GR limit, abandons strict perturbative renormalizability); or curvature-squared quadratic gravity in the Stelle / Fradkin-Tseytlin class, ideally with the Anselmi-Piva fakeon prescription quantizing the massive spin-2 mode as a fakeon rather than a ghost (preserves perturbative renormalizability, abandons exact TEGR closure).

## New context

Three pieces of public-record context surfacing since the original paper have not yet been integrated into the assessment above.

1. **Author follow-up — graviton-graviton extension.** arXiv:2507.07790 (Partanen-Tulkki, July 2025) extends the original construction by introducing a graviton-graviton interaction term L_gg,int = −i ∑_a T_g^{aν} I_g^{a*} 𝒟_ν I_g^a via a gravitational stress-energy tensor T_g^{μν} = (1/2κ) P^{μν,ρσλ,αβγ} ∂_ρ H_{σλ} ∂_α H_{βγ}, generating a triple-graviton vertex while preserving 4×U(1) gauge invariance. The total stress-energy tensor becomes T^{μν} = T_m^{μν} + T_g^{μν}. The paper analyzes the extension at classical / tree level only. Verbatim from the paper: *"Regarding the Feynman diagrams of UG, our extension introduces the triple-graviton vertex, whose implications to the renormalization of UG are left as a topic of further work."* The extension introduces no higher-derivative or torsion-quartic terms; it does not engage with prior critiques of the original construction.

2. **Author follow-up — light-deflection observational claim.** arXiv:2505.14446 (Partanen-Tulkki, May 2025) computes light deflection in unified gravity at second-post-Newtonian order using an iterative weak-field expansion with nonlinear graviton-graviton interaction included at lowest (tree) order. Verbatim: *"the second PN order terms of unified gravity reveal measurable relative differences of 7/30 ≈ 23.3% and 4/15 ≈ 26.7% for the out-of-plane and in-plane polarizations"* relative to general relativity. The LATOR (Laser Astrometric Test of Relativity) mission proposal is cited as a falsifiability route. Predicted relative differences range from ~10^{-8} (Jupiter) to 0.126 (neutron star RX J1856.5-3754). The paper contains no analysis of loop-level renormalization.

3. **Third-party critique — Barker spectrum analysis.** arXiv:2311.11790 (Will Barker, November 2023, "Particle spectra of gravity based on internal symmetry of quantum fields") examines the weak-field, zero-coupling limit of unified gravity viewed as a free quantum field theory. The abstract states: *"In this approximation the theory has a ghostly teleparallel vacuum. We suggest that bimetric, vacuum expectation value, or finite-coupling extensions should be investigated."* This is the only credentialed third-party arxiv critique of unified gravity found in 1.5+ years of public record since the original paper. Barker's broader 2024-2025 work (arXiv:2406.12826, 2505.23894, 2506.02111, 2510.08201) develops spectral-analysis machinery in related but distinct gauge-gravity classes; none of these later papers re-engage Partanen-Tulkki by name.
