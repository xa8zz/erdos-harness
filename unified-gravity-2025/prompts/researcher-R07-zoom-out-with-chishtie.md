## The construction

A candidate quantum theory of gravity ("unified gravity") was proposed by Partanen and Tulkki in arXiv:2310.01460 (Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 057802, 2025; two published corrigenda — Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 069501, 2025 corrects a factor-of-2 error in the projector tensor P^{μν,ρσ,ηλ} prefactor across five equations including eq. 162; and 88, 079501, 2025 corrects an eq. 122 factor 1/2 → 1 with no downstream impact; both folded into arxiv v11). The construction is a U(1)^4 abelian gauge theory with eight-spinor matter coupling and a teleparallel-superpotential kinetic term L_{g,kin} = (1/4κ) H_{ρμν} S^{ρμν} √(-g), claimed to be one-loop renormalizable in the standard Bogoliubov-Parasiuk-Hepp-Zimmermann sense, reducing to teleparallel general relativity in the Weitzenböck gauge under a gauge-field-dependent geometric condition.

The program's stated goals are: (i) perturbative quantum theory of gravity on the same footing as Standard Model gauge interactions; (ii) classical reduction to general relativity via the TEGR equivalence; (iii) compatibility with the equivalence principle through equality of renormalized inertial and gravitational masses.

## What has been established about the strict claim

The headline one-loop renormalizability claim does not survive within the strict original parameter set. Three independent obstructions, paper-citation-verified against arxiv:2310.01460v11:

**Obstruction A — matter-loop higher-derivative + axial-current-quartic counterterms.** Spinor matter loops generate a Weyl-squared / four-derivative (∂F)^2 counterterm of mass dimension 6 in gauge variables, with Duff coefficient b = 1/20 per Dirac fermion (Duff 1994, Class. Quantum Grav. 11, 1387). The Einstein-Dirac one-loop sector additionally generates an irreducible axial-current quartic (ψ̄ γ^a γ_5 ψ)^4 — a dimension-12 eight-spinor operator (Deser & van Nieuwenhuizen 1974, Phys. Rev. D 10, 411). Both are BRST-closed in ghost-number-0 cohomology (Barnich-Brandt-Henneaux 2000, Phys. Rep. 338, 439, arXiv:hep-th/0002245) and absent from the bare Lagrangian.

**Obstruction B — paper-internal counterterm-basis mismatch.** The one-loop graviton self-energy 1PI two-point tensor (eqs. 187-188 of arXiv:2310.01460v11) has overall scaling κ p^4 / ε_UV. The electron-loop divergence decomposes into P̂_{3,2,1} p^4 − 10 m_e^2 c^2 p^2 P̂_{1,2,1} − 30 m_e^4 c^4 P_{1,0,1}, with three independent tensor projectors. The bare-action counterterm structure (eqs. 157, 161, 162, 189) is restricted to (Z_H − 1) p^2 P̂_{1,2,1} with constant Z_H — one projector, p^2 only. The paper's resolution introduces a momentum-dependent δZ_H^{(1)}(p^2) (eqs. 203-205) of explicit nonpolynomial form depending on p^2, m_e^2/p^2, m_e^4/p^4, and logarithms, satisfying subtraction conditions Ξ_{1L}|_{p^2 = 0} = 0 and (Ξ_{1L}/p^4)|_{p^2 → ∞} = 0. The structural reading: this corresponds either to enlargement of the local counterterm basis with new higher-derivative operators (exiting power-counting renormalizability) or to nonlocal propagator-level subtraction outside the BPHZ class.

**Obstruction C — teleparallel matter-loop divergences outside the TEGR one-parameter family.** Per Casadio, Kuntz, Paci (Eur. Phys. J. C 82, 186, 2022; arXiv:2110.04325), quantum matter loops on a teleparallel background generate one-loop divergences in two structural forms depending on coupling prescription. Under full equivalence-principle coupling (translation + local Lorentz), divergences are quartic in torsion. Under gauge-only translation coupling, divergences are quadratic in torsion in the Yang-Mills contraction T^{ρμν} T_{ρμν} alone, not in the TEGR linear combination (1/4) T_1 + (1/2) T_2 − T_3. Absorption requires enlargement to a three-parameter "New GR" teleparallel action with independent coefficients for the three torsion-quadratic invariants.

BRST does not save the claim: the offending counterterms are local, Lorentz-invariant, gauge-invariant, and BRST-closed in ghost-number-0 cohomology. The "dimensionless coupling" claim relies on a √κ field rescaling H_{aν} → √κ H'_{aν} that hides the dimensionful coupling in the interaction vertices; the effective expansion parameter is α_g = G p^2 / (ℏ c^3), explicitly energy-scale dependent — characteristic of effective field theory rather than fixed dimensionless BPHZ coupling.

Structural impossibility: within the strict original family — local algebraic-renormalization framework, U(1)^4 abelian gauge structure, exact TEGR closure under renormalization, and a finite bare coupling set — no local-BPHZ-renormalizable descendant can absorb A, B, C while preserving all three stated goals. Two coherent descendants exist within known physics: (1) a teleparallel/tetrad effective field theory with full local higher-derivative + torsion-quadratic + torsion-quartic + axial-current-quartic operator basis (preserves classical GR limit, abandons strict perturbative renormalizability — the Donoghue-Burgess EFT route); (2) curvature-squared Stelle / Fradkin-Tseytlin quadratic gravity, ideally with the Anselmi-Piva fakeon prescription quantizing the massive spin-2 mode as a fakeon rather than a ghost (preserves perturbative renormalizability, abandons exact TEGR closure and standard microcausality above the fakeon scale).

## What has been established about the discourse and follow-ups

The construction has split into two structurally distinct branches in the public record. The authors' July 2025 follow-up (arXiv:2507.07790, *Extending unified gravity to account for graviton-graviton interaction*) explicitly states: (a) there is no equivalence transformation from unified gravity to TEGR; (b) the substitutions that recover TEGR break the gauge symmetry of unified gravity; (c) the new gauge-invariant graviton-graviton self-interaction term ℒ_{gg,int} = −i ∑_a T_g^{aν} I_g^{a*} 𝒟_ν I_g^a does not contribute to the TEGR-reduced Lagrangian. So the program has split into (i) a gauge-invariant Minkowski-UG branch (carrying the new triple-graviton vertex) and (ii) a TEGR-reduced branch (recovered only after symmetry-breaking substitutions). The two are not equivalent presentations of one theory.

The May 2025 follow-up (arXiv:2505.14446, *Light deflection in unified gravity and measurable deviation from general relativity in the second post-Newtonian order*) predicts polarization-dependent deviations from general relativity at second post-Newtonian order — verbatim, *"the second PN order terms of unified gravity reveal measurable relative differences of 7/30 ≈ 23.3% and 4/15 ≈ 26.7% for the out-of-plane and in-plane polarizations"* — tree-level only, citing the LATOR mission concept as an experimental test. Context: in conventional metric electrodynamics coupled to general relativity, classical geometric-optics light propagation is not polarization-split; the known polarization-dependent gravitational propagation arises from QED loop vacuum-polarization in curved background (Drummond & Hathrell 1980, Phys. Rev. D 22, 343), not classical 2PN gravitational deflection. So the unified-gravity prediction belongs to the Minkowski-UG branch and is a genuinely non-GR classical signature.

The July 2025 graviton-graviton extension states explicitly: *"Regarding the Feynman diagrams of UG, our extension introduces the triple-graviton vertex, whose implications to the renormalization of UG are left as a topic of further work."* This is a direct concession of the loop-level renormalization gap.
