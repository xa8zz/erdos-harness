## Background

The paper "Gravity generated by four one-dimensional unitary gauge symmetries and the Standard Model" by Mikko Partanen and Jukka Tulkki (Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 057802, 2025; arXiv:2310.01460) presents a U(1)^4 gauge theory of gravity ("unified gravity") with four abelian gauge connections A^a_μ (a = 0,1,2,3; the paper may use alternative notation such as H^a_{μν} for the field strength), claimed to be a dynamical gauge theory of gravity in the same sense as Yang-Mills theory is a dynamical gauge theory of the Standard Model interactions.

The task below evaluates a specific structural claim about the gravitational kinetic term of this theory.

## Claim to verify or refute

The gravitational kinetic term in the bare Lagrangian of the paper has the Lorentz-tensor structure of an "instanton density" / Pontryagin density — schematically of the form

ε^{μνρσ} F^a_{μν} F^b_{ρσ}

(possibly contracted with some flavor/index structure between the four U(1) factors, or expressed in the paper's field-strength notation H^a_{μν} or analog) — rather than the standard Yang-Mills structure

F^a_{μν} F^{a μν}

(equivalent to E^2 − B^2 in the standard electric/magnetic decomposition, or equivalently the Lorentz scalar bilinear in field strengths that is **not** the dual contraction).

In electric/magnetic notation, the instanton-density structure is proportional to E · B, whereas the Yang-Mills structure is proportional to E^2 − B^2.

**Implication if the claim is true:** for an abelian gauge field, the integrand ∫ d^4x ε^{μνρσ} F_{μν} F_{ρσ} is exactly the total divergence of the Chern-Simons current K^μ = ε^{μνρσ} A_ν ∂_ρ A_σ, hence the corresponding action contributes zero to the Euler-Lagrange equations of motion. Such an action is topological (counts winding number / second Chern class), not dynamical, and the gauge field has no propagator generated by it. Mixing with other gauge-invariant terms could lift the action away from being purely topological; the question is whether such mixing exists and whether it generates a non-degenerate kinetic term for the gravitational gauge field.

**Implication if the claim is false:** the kinetic term is of the standard Yang-Mills (F^2) form and the gauge field has a standard propagator generated by it.
