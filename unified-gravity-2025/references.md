## Foundational works (pre-seeded)

These are the textbook / canonical references most likely to be cited repeatedly. Pre-seeded so the first dispatch can already cite them; expand as rounds complete.

- **[Partanen & Tulkki 2025]** Gravity generated by four one-dimensional unitary gauge symmetries and the Standard Model. *Rep. Prog. Phys. 88, 057802; arXiv:2310.01460.* The paper under evaluation; baseline for R01-paper-intake.
- **['t Hooft & Veltman 1974]** One-loop divergencies in the theory of gravitation. *Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré 20, 69.* Pure gravity at one loop on-shell finite (Gauss-Bonnet topological); matter coupling breaks even on-shell finiteness.
- **[Goroff & Sagnotti 1986]** The ultraviolet behavior of Einstein gravity. *Nucl. Phys. B 266, 709.* Pure gravity two-loop divergent ∝ R³ on-shell counterterm.
- **[Stelle 1977]** Renormalization of higher-derivative quantum gravity. *Phys. Rev. D 16, 953.* Higher-derivative gravity all-orders renormalizable but contains a massive spin-2 ghost.
- **[Becchi, Rouet, Stora 1976]** Renormalization of gauge theories. *Ann. Phys. 98, 287.* BRST cohomology framework; Slavnov-Taylor identities.
- **[Bogoliubov & Parasiuk 1957 / Hepp 1966 / Zimmermann 1969]** BPHZ subtraction scheme. *Acta Math. 97, 227 / Commun. Math. Phys. 2, 301 / Commun. Math. Phys. 15, 208.* Standard subtraction scheme for perturbative renormalization.
- **[Connes & Kreimer 2000]** Renormalization in quantum field theory and the Riemann-Hilbert problem I/II. *Commun. Math. Phys. 210, 249; 216, 215; arXiv:hep-th/9912092, hep-th/0003188.* Hopf-algebra structure of Feynman graphs underlying BPHZ.
- **[Aldrovandi & Pereira 2013]** *Teleparallel Gravity: An Introduction.* Fundamental Theories of Physics 173, Springer. TEGR equivalence to GR, Weitzenböck connection.
