## Setup

For \(n\ge2\), two players alternately choose previously unchosen integers from \(\{2,\ldots,n\}\), keeping the chosen set an antichain under divisibility. The game ends when the set is maximal. Prolonger moves first and maximizes the number of moves; Shortener minimizes it; \(L(n)\) is the optimal length. Known: \(c_\delta n(\log\log n)^2/\log n\le L(n)\le(0.1897123371+o(1))n\).

## Established facts (complete proofs exist; take them as given)

1. Reduction: \(L(n)=o(n)\) follows from a shell statement — there is \(h(n)\to\infty\) with \(|Q_X(\mathrm{end})|\le\tfrac12|Q_X(\mathrm{start})|+X/h^2\) per processing block uniformly over dyadic shells under an explicit Shortener policy, via a trapping sweep, geometric shell summation, and static completion (a primitive \(P\), \(|P|=o(n)\), has \(o(n)\) minimal legal elements). Equivalently sufficient: a repair potential whose increase under one chronological blocker is controlled by the blocker's CONTEMPORANEOUS degree area, not its lifetime descendant multiplicity.
2. First-death normal form: if a face \(d\) stays legal while its lift \(dp\) is dead, the first comparable move is \(a=ep\), \(e\mid d\), an irreversible actual-state event; one actual move cannot destroy two distinct prime lifts of the same legal face; traces incident to one face have lcm dividing it. Canonical cube partition: writing \(d=c(d)\prod_{j\in S(d)}\ell_j\) with all \(\ell_j\)-valuations of \(c(d)\) even partitions the integers into rank-\(r\) cubes; a face integer belongs to exactly one cube; the sequential Boolean history over one core reveals \(2^r\) signatures (lifetime factor \(2^r\), not the antichain width).
3. Atomic ledger: pre-registering all core-signature faces as latent objects yields the lifetime bound: faces whose blocker record ever contains \(c\) number at most \(2^r\tau(c)\) for the residual channel oriented by \(\kappa(F)\mid c\). Direct ancestors (\(c\mid a_F\)) escape every bound of the form \(M\cdot\tau(c)\): one move can erase whole lift families of \(H^{\Omega(1)}\) unmergeable faces with \(\tau(c)\) constant.
4. Anchor exploit: against the LITERAL maximum-current-degree policy, Prolonger opens with \(B_H=\operatorname{lcm}(2,\ldots,H)\) (killing every integer in \([2,H]\)), then plays lift primes \(p\in(H,2H]\); provably no legal proper multiple of an unplayed \(p\) is ever a maximum-degree move (reserved semiprime targets \(pr\) give \(\deg(p)>\deg(pm)\)), so within \(O(H/\log H)\) moves \((1-o(1))\) of a shell's faces have every lift first-witnessed at trace 1. The exploit is NOT robust: a single legal batch blocker \(\prod_{H<p\le2H}p\le n^{1/12}\) kills every lift prime at once, with witness lcm so large that the affected face density is \(e^{-\Theta(H)}\); the literal maximum-degree rule refuses this move only because each individual prime out-degrees it.
5. Failed normalizations, with mechanisms: (a) raw all-witness low-lcm sums are inflated by redundant re-witnessing of dead lifts (semiprime grids give \(R^s\) tuples from \(Rq\) moves); (b) first-witness tuples indexed by MOVE indices are ill-defined — the same move can first-witness different tags with different traces for different faces (move \(35=5\cdot7\): faces \(77\) at tag \(5\) with trace \(7\), and \(65\) at tag \(7\) with trace \(5\)); (c) any valid tuple normalization must be indexed by face-tag pairs and must carry a no-reuse statement that survives later batches; (d) per-snapshot pairwise cross-carrier cleanups do not globalize to chronological injectivity for free; (e) iterating static completion across \(\log_2 H\) growing-depth minimal layers is a growing-depth uniformity problem the fixed-prefix theorem does not cover; (f) random-witness repairs lose an unavoidable \(2^r/\binom r{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}\asymp\sqrt r\) temporal factor.

## Statement to prove or disprove

There exists a fully specified, legal, online Shortener policy (every turn defined, including fallback moves and batch blockers such as products of lift primes) together with a chronological inequality valid uniformly over arbitrary Prolonger play against it — in any equivalent form: a residual-weighted moment bound \(\frac1Y\sum_{f\ \mathrm{legal}}\binom{k_T(f)}{s}=o(\binom{E_H}{s})\) for some \(s\); an ancestor-harvest bound (first-exit direct-ancestor mass \(\le o(X)+O(Ht)\) until the degree cap); or a contemporaneous-area repair potential — strong enough to give the shell statement in fact 1 and hence \(L(n)=o(n)\). A disproof must show every such policy admits a Prolonger exploit persisting \(\asymp X/H\) rounds with the relevant residual mass of full order — not merely one stopping time, and not merely against one policy. Either outcome decides the sublinear side of the problem; do not force one. If neither closes, state the single sub-step that cannot be closed.
