## Setup

For \(n\ge2\), two players alternately choose previously unchosen integers from \(\{2,\ldots,n\}\), keeping the chosen set an antichain under divisibility. The game ends when the set is maximal. Prolonger moves first and maximizes the number of moves; Shortener minimizes it; \(L(n)\) is the optimal length. Known: \(c_\delta n(\log\log n)^2/\log n\le L(n)\le(0.1897123371+o(1))n\).

## Established facts (complete proofs exist; take them as given)

1. Absorbing fortress: once a position with residual degree cap \(\Delta_Q\le H\) and a linear trapped reservoir is reached, it is stable against arbitrary play — blocked small divisors remain illegal forever, the cap is monotone along every continuation, and the remaining game value is at least \(|E|+|Q|/H\) (exact-only targets are banked actual moves). Static reachability of such positions is proved (primorial batching: a sublinear family of upper-half batched carriers gives \(|Q|=(\tfrac12-o(1))n\), \(\Delta\le H\) slowly growing).
2. Entry at growing cap \(H(n)\to\infty\) yields only \(L(n)\ge n^{1-o(1)}\), compatible with \(L(n)=o(n)\). A linear lower bound \(L(n)\ge cn\) requires entry at a FIXED cap \(H=O(1)\), or an amplification converting a growing-cap fortress into a fixed-cap conclusion without losing a linear number of targets. Histories that beat the maximum-degree or minimal-legal policies do not lower-bound the game: neither policy is proved dominant; a bound must withstand arbitrary replies.
3. Fresh-batch persistence: if at activation every legal face of a rank-\(r\) primorial batch has at least \(2\binom r{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}+1\) legal prime lifts, Prolonger clears the batch in at most \(\binom r{\lfloor r/2\rfloor}\) carrier moves against arbitrary subsequent replies. Failure is only by advance depletion.
4. Anti-entry tools available to Shortener: exhausting every prime lift of one legal face requires \(\Omega(H/\log H)\) distinct earlier moves; one legal batch blocker (a product of an interval of primes, size \(n^{o(1)}\)) kills an entire lift-prime family at once at face-density cost \(e^{-\Theta(H)}\); a single legal lower move can eliminate hundreds of live upper targets (reservoir sacrifice); per-shell witness counting gives: faces with \(q\) blocked tags satisfy \(|\mathcal F|\binom qs\le 2Y\sum 1/\mathrm{lcm}\) over distinct-tag witness \(s\)-tuples, and high-lcm tuples are negligible.
5. Prolonger-side tools: the anchor \(B_H=\operatorname{lcm}(2,\ldots,H)\le e^{(1+o(1))H}\) makes every integer in \([2,H]\) illegal in one move; ordered decreasing-product spoiler grids \(a_t=r_tp_t\) can hit arbitrarily many faces per spoiler at exponentially small density; a rank-\(r\) divisor cube supports coded and product-escalating repairs of frozen blocker configurations (single middle-rank cross-spoiler: normalized reciprocal repair weight \(\le2/q_1\); diagonal semiprime matchings: logarithmic coded repairs). At fixed \(r\) and fixed \(H\), all binomial and primorial factors are \(O(1)\).

## Statement to prove or disprove

There is a constant \(H_0\) and \(c>0\) such that Prolonger, against ARBITRARY legal Shortener play, can force reaching a position with residual degree cap \(\Delta_Q\le H_0\) and trapped reservoir of size at least \(cn\) — OR there is an amplification theorem converting the growing-cap entry (granted at any \(H(n)\to\infty\) if you need it) into \(L(n)\ge c'n\). A proof of either resolves the problem's lower-bound side: \(L(n)=\Theta(n)\) up to the known upper constant. A disproof must show a Shortener strategy that, against every Prolonger plan, either breaks every fixed cap (forces \(\Delta_Q>H_0\) resolution) or destroys the reservoir to \(o(n)\) — quantified against the batch-blocker and reservoir-sacrifice tools above. Either outcome is decisive for its side; do not force one. If neither closes, state the single sub-step that cannot be closed.
